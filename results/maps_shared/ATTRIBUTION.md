# Attribution for the KITTI-based figures

The figures in this folder show pictures from the KITTI Vision Benchmark Suite (2D object detection, left colour images): A. Geiger, P. Lenz and R. Urtasun, "Are we ready for Autonomous Driving? The KITTI Vision Benchmark Suite", CVPR 2012, https://www.cvlibs.net/datasets/kitti/

The KITTI data is under [CC BY-NC-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/3.0/). I scaled the pictures and drew a coloured 10 x 3 grid, numbers and a green box on them.
